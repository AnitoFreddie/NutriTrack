    How to record your food
    1. Log right after you eat, so you don't forget.
    2. Pick the meal, then search the food by its common name.
    3. Check the serving size shown, then enter how many servings you ate. Half a cup of rice is 0.5.
    4. Log everything: snacks, drinks, sauces, and cooking oil.
    5. Mixed dishes count by the dish. Log each part separately if you can, such as rice and ulam.
    6. If a food isn't listed, add it under "Add a food that isn't in the list" using its label.
    7. Log every day, even on busy days. Consistent records give better feedback.
